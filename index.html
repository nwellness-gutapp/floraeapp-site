What florae does

florae is a gut health tracker for iPhone. Log what you eat, how you feel and the habits you keep, and florae shows you how they connect over time.

Daily gut-habit score

A simple score out of 100 for how your day is going, so you can see at a glance where you stand.

Food and fibre diary

Record meals in seconds and follow your fibre intake against a daily goal.

Gut and mind symptoms

Note bloating, cramping, reflux and more, plus mood-related symptoms, with a severity rating and a short note.

Habits and streaks

Build small daily habits such as drinking water, meditating, breathwork and avoiding late-night eating.

Trends and charts

See fibre intake and symptom severity across the week side by side.

Ask in plain language

Tell florae what happened or ask a gut health question, and it helps you log it.

Please note

florae is a tracking and wellbeing tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition and is not a substitute for advice from a doctor or other qualified health professional. If you are worried about your health, speak to one.
